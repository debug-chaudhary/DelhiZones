📚 General Guidelines for All BCA Practical Courses

Before diving into specific semester BCA lab courses, it's crucial to understand the universal rules that apply to all BCA practical components throughout the programme. These guidelines are mandatory for every student enrolled in the BCA programme and must be followed strictly to ensure eligibility for the BCA practical exam.

⚠️

Compulsory Attendance

You must have a minimum of 70% attendance for BCA practical counselling sessions to be eligible to appear for the Term-End Practical Examination. This is non-negotiable.

📊

Evaluation Components

BCA lab courses are evaluated on two components: Continuous Assessment (25%) and Term-End Practical Exam (75%). Both are mandatory.

✅

Passing Criteria

You must score at least 40% in each section of the BCA practical course to pass. Failing any section means repeating the entire practical.

📅 BCA Practical Attendance Requirements

Attendance for BCA lab courses is strictly monitored and enforced. Unlike theory courses where counselling is optional, BCA practical sessions require mandatory physical presence at designated study centers.

⚠️ Critical Attendance Rules

  • → Minimum 70% attendance is compulsory for all BCA practical sessions
  • → Attendance is tracked for each individual BCA lab course separately
  • → Falling below 70% makes you ineligible for the practical examination
  • → No exceptions are made except for medical emergencies with proper documentation
  • → You must re-register for the course if attendance falls short

✅ Attendance Calculation Example

Total Sessions: 10 practical classes
Minimum Required: 7 classes (70%)
Maximum Absences: 3 classes only
Status: Eligible for exam

❌ What Happens If You Miss

  • → Cannot appear in practical exam
  • → Must re-register for the course
  • → Delays programme completion
  • → Additional fees for re-registration

⚖️ BCA Practical Evaluation System Explained

Understanding the BCA practical evaluation system is crucial for scoring well in BCA lab courses. The evaluation is divided into two major components, each carrying specific weightage.

25%

Continuous Assessment

Internal evaluation throughout the semester consisting of:

Lab Records 40 marks

Maintain complete practical notebooks

Assignment Problems 40 marks

Solve assigned practical problems

Viva-Voce 20 marks

Oral examination on concepts

75%

Term-End Practical Exam

Final practical examination conducted at term-end:

Practical Questions 80%

Hands-on problem solving on computer

Viva-Voce 20%

Oral questions during/after exam

Duration 2-3 hours

Varies by course complexity

🎯 Passing Requirements

Minimum in Each Component:

40% in Continuous Assessment AND 40% in Practical Exam

Overall Aggregate:

40% aggregate marks combining both components
