1

Smaaash (Dwarka & Vasant Kunj)

🏏 Best For: Cricket Lovers & VR Enthusiasts

If you're searching for the ultimate game zone in Delhi, Smaaash is usually the first name that pops up. It's famous for its revolutionary 360-degree cricket simulator where you can face legendary bowlers in an immersive experience.

📍 Locations Ambience Mall (Vasant Kunj) & Radisson Blu (Dwarka)
💰 Price Range ₹800 - ₹1,500 per person
⏰ Timings 11:00 AM - 11:00 PM (Daily)
🎯 Vibe High energy, perfect for parties

🌟 Key Attractions:

  • Twilight Bowling
  • VR Jurassic Park
  • Finger Coaster
  • Cockpit 360
  • Virtual Cricket
  • Walk the Plank VR

Pro Tip: Visit during weekdays to avoid weekend crowds and get better rates on combo packages!

📍 Location JMD Empire Square, Gurgaon
💰 Price Range ₹1,500 - ₹3,000 per session
⏰ Timings 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM
🎯 Vibe Futuristic & intense

🌟 Key Attractions:

  • Zero Latency VR (Zombie Survival)
  • Full-Body VR Experiences
  • Golf Simulators
  • Car Racing Simulators
  • Multi-player Arena
  • F1 Racing Pods
