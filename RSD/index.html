Simple Installation

1

Prep

Turn off the main water valve. Ensure the rubber washers are seated inside the nuts. No Teflon tape needed.

2

Connect

Screw one end to the wall angle valve and the other to your appliance (Geyser/Tap). Hand tighten first.

3

Secure

Use a wrench to tighten an extra 1/4 turn. Turn on water slowly to check for leaks.
