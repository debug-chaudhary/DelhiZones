Level Up! Top 10 Gaming Zones in Delhi (2026)

From free-roaming VR arenas to chill bowling lounges, we've curated the ultimate list of entertainment spots for adults and families in the capital.

10 Premium Zones
1.2k+ Monthly Reads
4.8 Avg Rating

Are you tired of the same old movie-and-dinner routine? You're not alone. Delhi's entertainment scene has exploded in 2026, offering high-tech experiences that go far beyond basic arcade games. Whether you are planning a bachelor party, a corporate offsite, or just a fun weekend, here are the top spots to visit.

#1

Smaaash

Best for Cricket & VR

The undisputed king of gaming in Delhi. Famous for its 360-degree cricket simulator where you face legends like Malinga and Warne.

Dwarka & Vasant Kunj
₹800 - ₹1,500/person

Key Attractions:

Twilight Bowling VR Jurassic Park Finger Coaster
#2

Yes Minister

Best "Barcade" Vibe

A nightlife staple at Essex Farms. Perfect for adults who want to sip craft cocktails while playing bowling or riding a mechanical bull.

Essex Farms, Hauz Khas
Open till 1:00 AM
#3

The Game Palacio

Luxury Gaming

Arguably the swankiest gaming zone in Delhi. Combines boutique bowling lanes with fine dining. Highly instagrammable.

📍 Ansal Plaza • Price: ₹1,500+

#4

Microgravity

Tech Marvel

World-class free-roaming VR technology. Play zombie survival games where you actually walk around the arena. Worth the drive to Gurgaon.

📍 JMD Empire, Gurgaon • Price: ₹1,500/session

#5

Timezone Gold

The reinvented classic. Now features Halo Fireteam Raven, Bumper Cars, and massive prize counters. Great for families.

📍 Multiple Malls • Price: ₹500+

#6

The Hidden Hour

Best Escape Room experience. Locked in a themed room (Metro Hijack, Prison Break), you have 60 mins to solve puzzles and escape.

📍 Hauz Khas & CP • Price: ₹800/person

#7

Glued Reloaded

A Noida favorite for private theater screenings and gaming. Offers pool tables, bowling, and a great food court in a casual setting.

📍 Noida Sector 41 • Price: ₹600+

#8

Mystery Rooms

Another top-tier escape room franchise. Famous for their horror-themed rooms like "The Conjuring" and "Ring". Not for the faint-hearted!

📍 CP, Rajouri Garden • Price: ₹900/person

#9

E-O-D Adventure Park

"Every Other Day" is a massive outdoor adventure park. Features bowling, boating, zip-lining, and an indoor arcade. Great for full-day outings.

📍 Mayur Vihar • Price: ₹400 Entry

#10

Barney's Den

A cozy, hidden gem in Hauz Khas. It's a gaming cafe perfect for PS5, PC gaming, and chilling with friends over burgers and shakes.

📍 Hauz Khas Village • Price: ₹300/hour
