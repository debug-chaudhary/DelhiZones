Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best zone for toddlers (1-3 years)?
Tumble House and Tingaland both have dedicated toddler zones. Tumble House is known for superior hygiene, while Tingaland offers more variety.
Is it safe during high pollution days?
Yes. Premium zones like Funky Li'l Munchkins and Tumble House use industrial air purifiers. KidZania is also fully air-conditioned and enclosed.
Do I need to book in advance?
For KidZania, advance booking is highly recommended on weekends. Most other venues accept walk-ins, but booking online often saves money.
