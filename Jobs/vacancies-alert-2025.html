📋 NREGA Job Card: Complete Guide

The NREGA job card (also known as MGNREGA job card or simply job card) is an essential document for accessing employment under the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. Check your nrega job card list, job card number, and work details through our comprehensive guide.

What is a Job Card?

A NREGA job card (or MGNREGA job card) is an official document issued to households under the employment guarantee scheme. The job card contains details of adult members who are willing to do unskilled manual work and is mandatory for getting employment under NREGA.

Key Features of Job Card NREGA:

  • ✓ Unique job card number for each household
  • ✓ Access to 100 days guaranteed employment
  • ✓ Transparent payment tracking system
  • ✓ State-wise job card list management
  • ✓ Digital nrega job card list access
